One look, taken from the two reference characters: flat colour, one even black line, white ground. Every screen, button and character in the game is built from what's on this page, and nothing else.

Principles

One line.

Every outline is the same ink black. UI lines are 2.5px; characters carry about 1% of their height.

Flat colour.

No gradients, glows or textures. Depth is a single ink step under things you can press.

White ground.

Characters and panels sit on white. Colour is for characters and actions, not backgrounds.

Faces lead.

Every character: small eyes perched on a big red nose. Never a character without a nose.

The cast

One drawer makes everyone. Two body types from the references (the tall bean and the round egg), one colour each, the same face. No hats, no props, except the boulder.
