    Add Rerack to your iPhone share button
    1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap + and name the shortcut Rack it.
    2. Tap the ⓘ button, turn on Show in Share Sheet, and let it receive URLs and Text.
    3. Add Copy to Clipboard with Shortcut Input.
    4. Add Open URLs with this link: https://claude.ai/artifact/Dgh14Ha89eTYWcSVVRApe8
    5. In Instagram or YouTube, tap Share → Rack it. Rerack opens. Tap the link box, Paste, tag the muscles, Save.

    Reminders: in Shortcuts go to Automation → Time of Day, choose your training days, and add Open URLs with the same link.
