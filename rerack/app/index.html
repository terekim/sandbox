    Share straight from Instagram
    1. Open Shortcuts, tap +, name it Rack it. Tap ⓘ and turn on Show in Share Sheet (receives URLs and Text).
    2. Add URL Encode with Shortcut Input.
    3. Add Open URLs with followed by the URL Encoded Text.
    4. In Instagram tap Share → Rack it. Rerack opens with the link filled in. Tag it and Save.

    For YouTube you don't need this: just tap Save → ReRack in YouTube.
