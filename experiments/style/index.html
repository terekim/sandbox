Characters canonical

Soft, hand-drawn-feeling creatures. No outlines, muted fills, tiny close-set eyes, one looping strand of hair. Live examples below (they breathe and blink):

jade · happy  ·  periwinkle · content  ·  clay · neutral  ·  lavender · sleepy  ·  rose · beaming (greeted)

Construction rules

  • No outlines, anywhere. Shapes are flat soft fills. The only black is face linework, the hair strand, and the hand hatch marks.
  • Body: one big organic blob (head and torso merged) with subtle hand-drawn "pulls" — gentle asymmetric bumps, never a clean geometric rounded-rect. The body dominates the silhouette.
  • Legs: short and thick, flaring into little splayed feet.
  • Arms: heavy, hanging down the sides, a half-step darker than the body, with 3 short hatch strokes for a hand.
  • Eyes: tiny, no whites, set close together near the upper third. A pair of small marks, not goggles.
  • Hair: one thin strand with a single loop that sits on top of the head. Always exactly one, never spiky, never a full head of hair.
  • Mouth: one minimal line. Small gentle curves, never a big grin or a hard frown.
  • Proportions: big soft body, short legs, small face high up. Weight low.

Expression range

Each character has a resting mood so a group never reads uniformly sad. Being greeted or warmed makes anyone briefly beam.

Do & don’t

Do

  • Keep lines soft and few
  • Muted, desaturated fills
  • Eyes small and close
  • One horizontal curl
  • Let posture and mood vary

    • Don’t

  • Black outlines on shapes
  • Neon or high-saturation color
  • Big white goggle eyes
  • Spiky or multiple hair
  • Everyone the same stance

    • Palette locked

    Soft and muted, no neons. Characters draw from these; arms are each colour at ~76% brightness.

    Environment in progress

    Now a soft illustrative world that shifts with the viewer’s local clock — dawn, midday, golden hour, dusk, night all blend across the day. Much closer to the characters; still being refined.

    one keyframe (dusk) of the day cycle
    • Keyframed sky by hour (9 stops) interpolated from the device time; e.g. dusk is periwinkle #7a76a6 → mauve #b58397 → apricot #e2a67e → sand #eccaa0.
    • A sun or moon arcs across the sky by the hour; stars fade in as the sky darkens.
    • Three layered hills, grass tufts and soft shrubs for landscape depth; the fire’s glow strengthens the darker it gets.
    • HUD text flips light/dark so it stays readable day or night.
    • Only a faint paper grain + vignette for warmth — characters stay crisp and flat (the heavier painted/wobble filter was pulled back).
    • A generated painted frame is kept as a mood, colour and environment-detail reference only, not the character or render style.
    • Open: trees and a distant lake, weather, foreground layering, and whether hero pieces become drawn assets.

    Fidelity & pipeline

    Everything here is currently code-built, which gets us “in the style of.” The real Moonsight-grade charm, the hand-made wobble and bespoke poses, comes from drawn assets. Plan: hero characters (and key environment props) get drawn as layered SVG in this exact style, and the rig animates them. Code keeps the systemic parts, fire, crowd behaviour, motion, the procedural world. This doc is the contract both sides build against.
